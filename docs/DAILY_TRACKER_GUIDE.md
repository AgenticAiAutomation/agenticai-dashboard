# Daily Tracker: how to use it

Open **dashboard.agenticaiautomation.co → Daily Tracker** (or the card on the homepage).

## Every day

1. **Today** tab → *Log your work*. Pick:
   - **Project:** only your own websites appear. If one is missing, ask Jai.
   - **Work type:** for example, Blog article published or Guest post.
   - **Proof link:** required. Paste the live URL of the work.
   - **Short note:** what you did, in one line. Required for "Other".
   - **Time spent** in minutes. **Date** is today.
2. Click **Log it**. It shows as *Pending review* until Jai checks it.
3. Your bar fills as work is **approved**. Hit your daily target and you get ✅, confetti
   and +1 to your streak.

## Rules that affect your marks

| Rule | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| Log it the same day | Logging yesterday's work today earns **half marks**. You can go up to 7 days back. |
| One link, one entry | The same link can't be logged twice, by anyone. |
| Backlinks | Links you add in **Backlink Ops** appear here on their own. Don't log them again. |
| Quality | Jai scores each item 1–5. 3 = full marks, 5 = 1.5×, 1 = nothing. |
| Needs fix | Click **Edit**, correct it, and it goes back for review without being counted as late. |

## Progress & leaderboard

Shows your week or month, a calendar (green = target hit), your streak, and your rank
this month: overall and on each of your websites.

## Idea Board: monthly bonus

Got an idea to grow your website? Click **Submit an idea**. Fill in the problem, your idea,
the result you expect and how to measure it. Jai may trial it. If it **works**, you get
the month's 🏆 winner badge and the bonus. Click 👍 on teammates' ideas you like.

## Tasks (once switched on)

The **My tasks** tab lists what Jai assigned you. Each task shows a colour:
- 🟢 green means on track.
- 🟠 amber means behind, or due within 24 hours and not submitted.
- 🔴 red means well behind, or overdue.

Backlinks, published blogs and work logged here count toward your tasks **by themselves**, so don't enter
anything twice. For other tasks, open the task and add progress with a proof link. When you're done, click
**Submit for review**. If something stops you, mark it **Blocked** and say why.

Every week you get one **idea task**, due Friday. Submit an idea on the Idea Board to complete it.

The **Mirror** tab shows where you stand:
- today, this week and this month against your targets
- your points and tier this month
- exactly what would get you to the next tier

## Backlink Ops levels

Your level is now **per website**: your own links and your own target on each site. The
homepage *My levels* card shows each site, your level, today's progress and your streak.
After 5 clean days in a row you show as *ready*, and Jai can raise you.

## If the dashboard is down

Log in the shared Google Sheet with the same columns (Email, Project, Work type, Link,
Note, Minutes, Date). Jai imports it later. Nothing is lost.
